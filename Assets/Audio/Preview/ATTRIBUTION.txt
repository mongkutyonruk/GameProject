Audio for GameProject
=====================
The chosen clips are copied into Assets/Resources/Audio and used by
AudioManager. This Preview folder is just the extras I listened to.

How to listen
-------------
1. Open this project in Unity (6000.3.22f1).
2. Select a file under Assets/Audio/Preview.
3. Press the play button at the bottom of the Inspector.

Suggested pairing (one from each folder)
----------------------------------------
Menu music     BGM/bgm_menu__wintery_loop.mp3
Gameplay music BGM/bgm_gameplay__christmas_synths.ogg
               (or BGM/bgm_alt__every_xmas_merry_xmas.ogg)
Present pickup SFX_Present/present_a__pleasing_bell.wav
Boost pickup   SFX_Boost/boost_a__confirmation.ogg
Boost end      SFX_Boost/boost_end_a__whoosh_down.ogg
Crash          SFX_Crash/crash_a__error.ogg
               (or crash_d__metal_hit.ogg if you want a physical hit)
UI click       SFX_UI/click_a__click.ogg
Pause / resume SFX_UI/pause__minimize.ogg
               SFX_UI/resume__maximize.ogg
